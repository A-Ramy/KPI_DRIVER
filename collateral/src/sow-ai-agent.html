Project objectives

Runs on your rules.

Hear it first: call +1 786-786-8382 and talk to our AI Agent.

This Statement of Work ("SOW") outlines the deployment of a bilingual (English and Spanish) AI Agent that handles voice and chat interactions for lead engagement, 24/7.

The objective is to automate pre-qualification of prospects, respond in real time and streamline appointment scheduling through conversational workflows. You set the guardrails once, and the agent decides when and how to follow up with new and older leads.

  • Channels: AI voice, SMS, email and iMessage
  • Bilingual communication, lead screening and automated booking
  • Number remediation and bring-your-own-carrier (BYOC)
  • Your team stays focused on high-value opportunities
