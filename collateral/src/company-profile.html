Company overview

Built by investors, for investors.

KPI DRIVER is a real estate business operations platform, developed over three years by investors, for investors. It brings every stage of the real estate cycle, from lead generation to post-closing, into one system.

Most CRMs stop at lead management. KPI DRIVER runs the whole operation, with dedicated pipelines for Acquisitions, Dispositions, Transaction Coordination, Contractor and Vendor Management, and Rental Oversight.

KPI tracking across all of it gives owners and managers clarity, control and the confidence to make data-backed moves. The AI Suite adds intelligence where it helps.
