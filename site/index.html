A connected operation

Most CRMs stop at the lead. Your business does not.

KPI DRIVER follows a deal through every team that touches it. Pick a stage to see what happens there and where the work goes next.

Capture

Every lead lands in one place

Leads from web forms, PPC campaigns, inbound calls, SMS, email and social arrive in a single acquisitions pipeline, so nothing lives in a spreadsheet or someone's inbox.

  • See every new lead in one organized view
  • Assign the lead to the right team member as it arrives
  • Keep the full history from first contact onward
Where it goes next
A new lead is either worked now or parked with a plan.
Common routes
Ready to talk → AcquisitionsNot ready yet → Nurture

Nurture

No lead goes cold by accident

Leads that are not ready today stay in a nurture pipeline with drip campaigns over SMS and email, matched to why the conversation stalled.

  • Build multi-touch follow-up campaigns
  • Trigger messages from status changes
  • Keep long-term follow-up running without extra admin
Where it goes next
When a seller re-engages, the lead moves back into active work.
Common routes
Seller responds → AcquisitionsStill waiting → stays in Nurture

Acquisitions

Work the seller, secure the property

Your acquisitions team negotiates terms and gets the property under contract, with offers, follow-ups and documents tracked against the deal.

  • Track negotiations and offers in one view
  • Set reminders for follow-ups and decision dates
  • Keep property details and documents with the deal
Where it goes next
A signed contract opens two tracks at once.
Common routes
Under contract → DispositionsUnder contract → Transaction CoordinationDeal stalls → Nurture

Dispositions

Market and sell the deal

Once a property is under contract, the dispositions pipeline covers marketing to agents, investors and buyers, showings, offers and the buyer agreement.

  • Send listings to your investor network
  • Track incoming offers and counteroffers
  • Schedule showings and log feedback
Where it goes next
A committed buyer hands the deal to coordination. Some deals you keep.
Common routes
Buyer secured → Transaction CoordinationKeeping it → Owned & Rentals

Transaction Coordination

From contract to close

Contracts, documents, payoffs and timelines are tracked through closing, with tasks assigned to the people responsible and documents signed inside the platform.

  • Send contracts for e-signature without leaving the system
  • Follow closing timelines and pending items
  • Keep every document linked to its deal
Where it goes next
Closing is not always the end of the record.

Contractors & Vendors

Repairs and vendor work, tracked

A dedicated pipeline for repairs, renovations and vendor tasks, tied to the deal or property the work belongs to.

  • Assign jobs and follow timelines
  • Track expenses against project budgets
  • Attach invoices, receipts and reports to each job

  • Track rental income and expenses per property
  • Follow lease start and end dates
  • Keep tenant communication and lease documents together

FAQ

Questions before the call

Not covered here? Send us a message.

What is KPI DRIVER?

KPI DRIVER is a real estate business operations platform built by investors for investors. Its CRM connects lead capture, nurture, acquisitions, dispositions, transaction coordination, contractors and vendors, and owned and rental properties, with KPI tracking across all of them.

What do I get with the Enterprise Optimization Suite?

The CRM and access to the AI Suite, together. There is no additional AI Suite subscription. Using the AI tools is optional, and you pay only for what you use.

Is the AI Agent included?

No. The AI Agent is a separate, optional offering. Teams that want it choose the Enterprise Optimization Suite + AI Agent. You do not need the AI Agent to use the CRM or the AI Suite.

How much does KPI DRIVER cost?

Pricing depends on your operation, so we cover it on a discovery call. Book a call and we will walk through fit, the two offerings, and how pay-as-you-go AI usage works.

Do I need separate systems for document signatures, dispositions, or rentals?

No. KPI DRIVER includes dedicated pipelines for each process, plus built-in document signature tools, so contracts stay attached to the deal they belong to.

Will KPI DRIVER work if I am new to real estate or do not have a CRM yet?

Yes. KPI DRIVER is designed for both new and established real estate businesses. New teams start with a streamlined setup. Established teams work with us on data migration and pipelines that match how they already operate.

Do you offer support and training?

Yes. Every client works with a dedicated account manager for onboarding and ongoing support, and our tech and support team is available for training, customization and strategy sessions.
