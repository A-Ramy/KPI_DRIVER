AI Agent Optional · separate offering

Runs on your rules.

Set the guardrails once, and the AI Agent decides when and how to follow up with new and older leads. No micromanaging. It is available with the Enterprise Optimization Suite + AI Agent.

What it does

Follow-up that does not depend on someone remembering.

Follow-up within configured rules

You define the guardrails. Within them, the agent chooses the timing and the approach for each follow-up.

New and older leads

It works fresh leads as they arrive and returns to older leads that have gone quiet.

Four listed channels

AI voice, SMS, email and iMessage. The channels used on your account are confirmed during setup.

Number remediation and BYOC

Listed capabilities for the phone numbers the agent uses, including bringing your own carrier. We explain how each applies to your setup on a discovery call.

How it fits

You set the rules. It does the follow-up.

The AI Agent is an addition to an operation that already runs on the CRM. Your pipelines, records and team stay where they are.

  1. Decide the guardrails

    Tell the agent how follow-up should work in your business.

  2. The agent follows up

    It reaches out to new and older leads across its channels, deciding when and how within your rules.

  3. Your team takes the conversation

    Engaged sellers are worked by your people in the CRM, as they are today.

Three things that sound similar

AI Agent, AI Suite and AI Nurture are not the same.

You do not need the AI Agent to use the AI Suite or AI Nurture.

FAQ

AI Agent questions

Related: AI Suite · CRM · Services overview

Is the AI Agent part of the AI Suite?

No. The AI Agent is a separate, optional offering available with the Enterprise Optimization Suite + AI Agent.

Can I try the AI Agent before a call with your team?

Yes. Call +1 786-786-8382 and talk to our AI Agent yourself. It is the quickest way to hear how it handles a conversation.

What does the AI Agent do?

It follows up with new and older leads within the rules you configure. You set the guardrails once, and it decides when and how to follow up within them.

Which channels does it use?

The listed channels are AI voice, SMS, email and iMessage. Channel availability for your account is confirmed during setup.

What are number remediation and BYOC?

They are listed AI Agent capabilities related to the phone numbers the agent uses. BYOC is short for bring your own carrier. We will explain how each applies to your numbers and carrier on a discovery call.

How is the AI Agent different from AI Nurture?

AI Nurture builds and schedules a tailored 60-day SMS and email campaign for a seller from your conversations and notes, and it does not require the AI Agent. The AI Agent is a separate offering that follows up with leads across its channels within your rules.

I already use the earlier AI Agent. What changes?

Existing AI Agent clients move to the new AI Agent through a migration. Your account manager will walk you through what it involves for your account.

What does the AI Agent cost?

AI Agent pricing is covered on a discovery call.
