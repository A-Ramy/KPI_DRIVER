Connected operational pipelines

Each team gets its own pipeline. The deal stays one record.

While most real estate CRMs stop at lead management and acquisitions, KPI DRIVER carries the deal through every handoff.

Capture

Every lead lands in one place

Leads from web forms, PPC campaigns, inbound calls, SMS, email and social arrive in a single acquisitions pipeline, so nothing lives in a spreadsheet or someone's inbox.

  • See every new lead in one organized view
  • Assign the lead to the right team member as it arrives
  • Keep the full history from first contact onward

Nurture

No lead goes cold by accident

Leads that are not ready today stay in a nurture pipeline with drip campaigns over SMS and email, matched to why the conversation stalled.

  • Build multi-touch follow-up campaigns
  • Trigger messages from status changes
  • Keep long-term follow-up running without extra admin

When a seller re-engages, the lead moves back into active work.
Common routes

  • Track negotiations and offers in one view
  • Set reminders for follow-ups and decision dates

Market and sell the deal

  • Send listings to your investor network
  • Track incoming offers and counteroffers
  • Schedule showings and log feedback

From contract to close

Contracts, documents, payoffs and timelines are tracked through closing, with tasks assigned to the people responsible and documents signed inside the platform.

  • Follow closing timelines and pending items
  • Keep every document linked to its deal

FAQ

CRM questions

Related: AI Suite · AI Agent · Services overview

Is the CRM sold on its own?

The CRM is the foundation of the Enterprise Optimization Suite, which also gives you access to the AI Suite. You choose whether to use the pay-as-you-go AI tools.

Which pipelines are built in?

Nurture, Acquisitions, Dispositions, Transaction Coordination, Contractors and Vendors, and Owned and Rentals. Each runs on its own pipeline and reports into the same KPI tracking.

Is the KPI dashboard customizable?

The KPI dashboard is pre-built around the metrics that matter in real estate investing, such as acquisitions, marketing performance and deal velocity. If you need something different, we can rework it with you.

Can my team work from a phone?

Yes. A mobile app for iOS and Android gives your team access to the CRM, conversations and tasks away from the desk.

Can you migrate my current CRM?

Yes. CRM migration is part of onboarding, with a focus on keeping your data intact. The plan and timing depend on your current system and data, so we scope it with you first.

How does the AI Suite relate to the CRM?

The AI Suite is a companion platform supplied alongside the CRM. It adds call capture and coaching, underwriting, skiptracing, data enrichment, dashboards, insights and projections on top of the operation your CRM already runs.
