Finish line

Pipelines converge

Three lanes (Lead, Deal, Property) that all pour into one Closed & Funded end cap.

  • Mirrors the CRM itself: each lane is a pipeline the client will actually see in the product.
  • The end cap is the boldest Closed & Funded of the three; nothing competes with it.
  • Taller diagram; on tablets the labels get smaller than in A.
OPTION C

Shift through the gears

A rev bar from red to green with numbered stages; Closed & Funded is top gear.

  • Most on-brand: uses the same red-to-green rev bar and gear numbers as the site's gear HUD and "Make the gear shift" line.
  • Numbers are real order: stages 1–5 run in sequence; the property work sits in a pit lane off the track.
  • Most stylised; simplest for a first-time visitor, least literal about loops between stages.
