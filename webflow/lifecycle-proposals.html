Redrawn around how the business actually runs. A signed contract forks into Dispositions and Transaction Coordination working side by side. The deal is either sold or kept, and a kept property comes back through the same pair when it sells. TC coordinates Contractors & Vendors the whole way through. Split gear track

Option C's rev bar, splitting into two lanes in the same gear, with a pit lane for property work.

  • Most on-brand: keeps the red-to-green rev bar and gear numbers from the site's gear HUD.
