The journey

From first lead to the property you keep.

The CRM covers the full real estate journey, each stage on its own pipeline, with document signing built in and KPI tracking over all of it.

Capture

Every lead lands in one place

Leads from web forms, PPC campaigns, inbound calls, SMS, email and social arrive in a single acquisitions pipeline, so nothing lives in a spreadsheet or someone's inbox.

  • See every new lead in one organized view
  • Assign the lead to the right team member as it arrives
  • Keep the full history from first contact onward
Where it goes next
New leads go straight to the people who close.
Common routes
New lead → Acquisitions

Acquisitions

Work the seller, secure the property

Your acquisitions team negotiates terms and gets the property under contract, with offers, follow-ups and documents tracked against the deal.

  • Track negotiations and offers in one view
  • Set reminders for follow-ups and decision dates
  • Keep property details and documents with the deal
Where it goes next
A signed contract opens two tracks at once. A stalled seller gets backup.
Common routes
Under contract → DispositionsUnder contract → Transaction CoordinationSeller stalls → Nurture takes over

Nurture

Backup for your acquisitions team

When a seller goes quiet or is not ready, Nurture steps in behind your acquisition managers. Drip campaigns over SMS and email, matched to why the deal stalled, keep working the lead until it comes back to life.

  • Takes stalled leads off your closers' plates
  • Situational, timed and AI-tailored nurtures, in English and Spanish
  • Hands revived leads back to acquisitions to close
Where it goes next
Nurture does not close deals. It revives them for the people who do.
Common routes
Seller re-engages → back to AcquisitionsStill quiet → Nurture keeps working

Dispositions

Market and sell the deal

Once a property is under contract, the dispositions pipeline covers marketing to agents, investors and buyers, showings, offers and the buyer agreement.

  • Send listings to your investor network
  • Track incoming offers and counteroffers
  • Schedule showings and log feedback

FAQ

How the offerings work

Are CRM, AI Suite and AI Agent three separate plans?

No. They are three product areas. There are two offerings: the Enterprise Optimization Suite (CRM plus AI Suite access) and the Enterprise Optimization Suite + AI Agent.

What does pay-as-you-go mean for the AI Suite?

Access to the AI Suite comes with the Enterprise Optimization Suite at no additional subscription. When you choose to use an AI tool, that usage is billed as you go. If you do not use the tools, there is no AI usage to pay for. Usage is tracked in a billing dashboard.

Can I use the CRM without using any AI tools?

Yes. AI Suite usage is optional. The CRM, pipelines, automations and KPI tracking work on their own.

What is scoped separately?

Work that depends on your setup is scoped on a call. Examples include custom automations, custom drip campaigns, custom landing pages, third-party integrations, Slack alerts and dedicated accounting support.

What does support look like after launch?

Every client has a dedicated channel with our team. You talk to a person, not a bot, and there is no ticket queue. Routine changes and edits are made at no extra cost. Substantial requests are quoted before any work starts.

Does KPI DRIVER integrate with my existing tools?

KPI DRIVER works alongside phone systems, marketing tools and third-party platforms. Our tech team scopes and handles integration setup with you, so tell us what you run today on your discovery call.

Where do I see pricing?

On your discovery call. We do not publish a rate card because the right setup depends on your team, your data and which AI tools you plan to use.
