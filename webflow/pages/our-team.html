At KPI DRIVER, we do not just build software. We engineer growth systems, and we use what we learned running a real estate investing business to do it.

Why we built it

Built by investors who know the road because they have driven it.

KPI DRIVER was engineered by real estate investors, marketers and technical people who needed a better way to run their own business. Over three years, our founders built and refined the processes, pipelines, KPI systems, drip campaigns and automations behind their own deals.

Most CRMs stopped at lead management. The business did not. So the platform grew to cover acquisitions, dispositions, transaction coordination, contractors and vendors, and rentals, with KPI tracking across all of it.

Our purpose is to give real estate professionals deep insight, streamlined processes and better decisions at every stage of the investment cycle, and to replace a clutter of disconnected tools with one integrated system. That work continues with the AI Suite.
